# Mark 4 Long-Range Cruiser (10-Inch) - Build & Tuning Specifications

This document outlines the complete hardware architecture, propulsion system, and Betaflight tuning parameters for the 10-inch Mark 4 endurance cruiser. The PID and filter configurations are specifically optimized for smooth, long-range flight dynamics and heavy payload stability, utilizing a custom STM32H743 flight controller.

## 1. Hardware & Propulsion Architecture
* **Frame:** Mark 4 (10-inch configuration)
* **Target AUW (All-Up Weight):** ~1.5 kg
* **Motors:** 950KV Brushless
* **Propellers:** 9450 
* **Power Source:** Custom 4S3P Lithium-Ion Pack (Samsung INR35E 18650 cells)
* **Flight Controller MCU:** DevEBox STM32H743VIT6 (ARM Cortex-M7 @ 480MHz)
* **Firmware:** Betaflight 2026.6.0-alpha (Custom Target)

## 2. Sensor & Avionics Package
* **Primary/Secondary IMU:** Dual BMI160 Gyro/Accelerometers (Running on independent SPI1 & SPI2 buses with dedicated DMA streams)
* **Barometer:** BMP280 (I2C)
* **Magnetometer:** QMC5883L (I2C, mapped for True North alignment)
* **GPS:** UBLOX M8 (UART6, 57600 baud)
* **Blackbox:** High-speed onboard SDIO MicroSD (FAT32, 32KB clusters) logging at 458Hz

---

## 3. PID Tuning Profile

These PID values are tailored for the high inertia of a 10-inch frame. D-gains are set slightly higher on Pitch to combat the larger moment of inertia, while Feedforward is kept moderate for smooth, cinematic stick feel.

**Basic / Acro**
* **ROLL:** P = 33 | I = 45 | D = 50 | D Max = 55 | FF = 30
* **PITCH:** P = 38 | I = 50 | D = 60 | D Max = 66 | FF = 30
* **YAW:** P = 35 | I = 60 | D = 25 | D Max = 30 | FF = 30

**PID Controller Settings**
* **I Term Relax:** On | Axes: RPY | Type: Setpoint | Cutoff: 15
* **Anti Gravity:** On | Gain: 8.0
* **Dynamic Damping:** Gain: 37 | Advance: 20
* **TPA:** Mode: D | TPA Rate: 65% | Breakpoint: 1350
* **Feedforward:** Jitter Reduction: 11 | Smoothness: 45 | Averaging: 2 Point | Boost: 15 | Max Rate Limit: 90
* **Throttle Boost:** 5
* **Vbat Sag Compensation:** 10%

---

## 4. Filter Settings

Given the lower frequency vibrations generated by 10-inch props, the global filter multipliers are shifted to prioritize cleaner gyro data over absolute minimal latency. 

**Global Multipliers**
* **Gyro Filter Multiplier:** 0.50
* **D Term Filter Multiplier:** 1.10

**Gyro Filters**
* **Gyro Lowpass 1:** STATIC | Cutoff: 125Hz | PT1
* **Gyro Lowpass 2:** STATIC | Cutoff: 250Hz | PT1
* **Dynamic Notch Filter:** Count: 3 | Q factor: 250 | Min Freq: 60Hz | Max Freq: 350Hz

**D-Term Filters**
* **D Term Lowpass 1:** DYNAMIC | Min Cutoff: 82Hz | Max Cutoff: 165Hz | Curve: 5 | PT1
* **D Term Lowpass 2:** STATIC | Cutoff: 165Hz | PT1
* **Yaw Lowpass Filter:** STATIC | Cutoff: 100Hz
